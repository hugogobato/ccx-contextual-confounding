# CCX Formalization Memo (WP 1.2)

Status: enabling formalization only (tag `enabling`, not paper theory). Purpose: fix the objects so WP 1.3 code is meaningless-free. Verified against AB 2011 full text (see `memos/prior_art_ledger.csv` for corrections absorbed here).

## 1. Scenario and structural classes

A **structure class** S is a DAG over nodes V = L ∪ O (latent L, observed O) with each endogenous node v carrying a mechanism v = f_v(pa(v)), together with a designated **context variable** Z ∈ O whose values index measurement contexts. Phase 1 uses binary alphabets throughout.

The running example is the binary IV class S_IV: latent U; observed Z, X, Y ∈ {0,1}; edges Z→X, X→Y, U→X, U→Y; mechanisms X = f(Z,U), Y = g(X,U). Randomization is Z ⫫ U; exclusion is the absence of a direct Z→Y edge. These are exactly Balke–Pearl's assumptions (JASA 1997, verified E3).

## 2. Response variables and the structured presheaf (F1/F2 fix)

For each endogenous node v define its **response coordinate** R_v = f_v(·, ·) as the function itself; the set of response tables is 𝕽_v = {val(v)}^{pa(v)}. For S_IV: R_X ∈ {0,1}^{{0,1}} has 4 tables (value of X at z=0, at z=1); R_Y ∈ {0,1}^{{0,1}} has 4 tables (value of Y at x=0, at x=1). Write 𝕽 = ∏_v 𝕽_v (|𝕽| = 16 for S_IV).

**Why the naive outcome sheaf is vacuous or wrong (records F1/F2).** If contexts are events {Z=z} and sections are couplings of the observed conditionals, a coupling always exists (product coupling), so contextuality never fires. Conversely, if outcomes are treated as globally shared variables across contexts, instrument relevance (P(X,Y|z) varying in z) makes the empirical model signaling and vanilla AB contextuality misfires. The fix, per the plan: sections live over **response tables**, not outcomes.

**Definition (structured presheaf).** Let Δ(𝕽) be the probability simplex over response tables (the latent alphabet is unrestricted in cardinality; a single latent of size |𝕽| suffices, and binary latents realize only two-point mixtures). For each context z let A_z ∈ {0,1}^{4×16} be the 0/1 matrix A_z[(x,y), (r_X, r_Y)] = 1[r_X(z) = x]·1[r_Y(r_X(z)) = y]. The pushforward π_z: Δ(𝕽) → Δ({0,1}²) is π_z(q) = A_z q. Any joint q over (U, (R_v)_v) realizes an observational law compatible with S, and conversely any q ∈ Δ(𝕽) is realized by taking U = the identity index of 𝕽; hence compatibility with S_IV at the level of P(x,y|z) (defined wherever P(z) > 0, which we assume throughout) is exactly membership in the image of Δ(𝕽). (This reduction is why a single unrestricted latent suffices: its only role is to couple the response coordinates arbitrarily.)

**Remark (naming).** The object above is a shared response space Θ = 𝕽 with per-context deterministic pushforwards, i.e., a hidden-variable model in the sense of AB's own factorization theorem rather than the AB event sheaf restricted to contexts. We keep the term "structured presheaf" for continuity with the plan; all Phase 1 statements are operationalized through the matrices A_z, so nothing depends on the categorical dressing.

**Definition (empirical model, structured global section, contextuality).** An empirical model is e = (e_z)_{z∈{0,1}}, each e_z ∈ Δ({0,1}²). A **structured global section** is q ∈ Δ(𝕽) with A_z q = e_z for both z. e is **S-contextual** iff no structured global section exists. Non-vacuity (it fires) and silence on feasible instances are checked exhaustively in WP 1.3.

**Remark (signaling wrinkle resolved).** Contexts reveal different response coordinates through the same matrices A_z; inconsistent connectedness across contexts is expected and permitted. Nothing requires e_0 = e_1. This is the F2 fix, in the spirit of CbD's explicit permitting of inconsistent connectedness.

## 3. Dictionary claims made precise (C1)

Let P_BP = {e = (e_0, e_1) : ∃q ∈ Δ(𝕽), A_z q = e_z ∀z}, a polytope of affine dimension 6 inside the product of two 3-simplexes. Its vertices are among the images A(δ_θ) of the 16 deterministic tables (12 distinct images); since the map is linear, vertices of P_BP are images of vertices of Δ(𝕽).

**Proposition 1 (Dictionary, adaptation-level).** e is S_IV-contextual iff (e_0, e_1) violates at least one facet inequality of P_BP, where the facet system is derived exactly (rational arithmetic after an exact affine-coordinate reduction, with qhull candidate generation and exact sign validation) from the vertex set. Numerical validation: agreement rate 100% at tolerance 1e-9 over the WP 1.3 enumeration (`t1_dictionary.csv`), with borderline band [1e-9, 1e-7] logged separately. Anchors: Balke–Pearl 1997 (sharp LP constraints, E3) and AB 2011 machinery (E3).

**Proposition 2 (Order-2 cross-check).** Consider the two-copy inflated system with shared Z: unknowns τ₁, τ₂ ∈ Δ(𝕽) and strictly positive weights P(z) > 0 for both z, subject to P(z)·A_zτ_i = P(z)·e_z (i = 1,2) plus the conditional independence of the copy responses given Z (automatic in the product form). The strict positivity of P(z) is essential: with zero mass allowed at a context, its constraints vanish and the system degenerates to per-context realizability, which is always true. Equivalence holds: (⇐) if q ∈ Δ(𝕽) is a structured global section, take τ₁ = τ₂ = q and any strictly positive P(z); (⇒) dividing out P(z) > 0 gives A_zτ_i = e_z exactly. Hence this check coincides with BP-feasibility: it is a validator of the primal LP and facet machinery, not an independent notion (with a shared mechanism law and no cross-copy observables, no inflation constraint beyond order-1 can bind). The implementation solves the post-conditioned system directly. (Logged in `t1_dictionary.csv` column `inflation2_feasible`.)

## 4. Hierarchy placement (C1b), adapted definitions

AB 2011 names the tiers possibilistic, probabilistic, and strong, and proves strong ⟺ maximal in their setting (their Prop 6.3, where maximal means noncontextual fraction zero; the faithful adaptation of that notion is t* = 0 of cf1_soft below, and the adapted equivalence support-contextual ⟺ t* = 0 holds because pointwise domination A_zw ≤ e_z keeps the residual a valid empirical model). Our adaptations route all quantifiers through the response pushforward:

**Possibilistic/strong (adapted).** e is **support-contextual** iff there is no q ∈ Δ(𝕽) with supp(π_z(q)) ⊆ supp(e_z) for both z. Equivalently cf1_hard < 1 (below): the equivalence holds because support containment is preserved under mixtures, so a compatible mixture exists iff a compatible deterministic table exists.

**All-context support-maximality (adapted, distinct from AB's maximal).** e is **all-context support-maximal** iff for every z and every q ∈ Δ(𝕽) there exists o ∈ supp(e_z) with π_z(q)(o) = 0. This quantifier structure strictly implies support-contextuality (its negation only requires one good q and one good z), so it is logically stronger; ME-B separates them (strongly contextual, yet not all-context support-maximal). Check: for each z solve max_{q∈Δ} m s.t. π_z(q)(o) ≥ m for all o ∈ supp(e_z); e has the property iff the optimum is m = 0 for both z. We track it because it is the strongest support-level obstruction available, while recording that it is NOT the adaptation of AB's maximal tier (which is t* = 0).

**cf1_hard** = max over the 16 deterministic tables θ of (1/2)·Σ_z 1[π_z(θ) ∈ supp(e_z)], the largest fraction of contexts jointly explained by one deterministic IV mechanism. Values lie in {0, 1/2, 1}.

## 5. Witness functionals (locked definitions)

All witnesses equal 0 (or their noncontextual value) on feasible instances, and are monotone candidates for "degree of contextuality"; the one exception is cf1_hard, whose noncontextual value 1 is attained exactly on possibilistically feasible instances (a strict superset of the feasible set). Normalizations are declared here and never changed.

1. **slack_l1** (distance to feasible set): min over q ∈ Δ(𝕽), auxiliaries u ≥ 0 of Σ_{z,o} u_{zo} s.t. u_{zo} ≥ ±(A_z q − e_z)_o. This is the L1 distance of e to P_BP. Remark (stipulative): the quantity (1/2)·slack_l1 is the minimal aggregate TV distance from e to a realizable law over honest couplings; we do not register it as a separate witness under that name because it is slack_l1 up to normalization. It is NOT the canonical CbD degree (which couples observable copies without structural constraints); no identification with the CbD measure is claimed.
2. **degree_signed** (CbD convention, signed quasi-couplings per de Barros–Kujala–Oas 2023 reading of CbD and Dzhafarov–Kujala): (1/2)·min over signed q with Σq = 1 of Σ_o u_o, u ≥ ±(Mq − e); equivalently the total-variation distance from e to the nearest quasi-coupling-realizable law. Warning locked here: signed quasi-couplings can achieve exact zero on contextual instances (micro-example B below), so degree_signed systematically under-reports relative to honest distances. Both conventions are reported and never mixed.
3. **cf1_soft** (probabilistic fraction, AB weighting style): t* = max Σ_{θ} w_θ s.t. w ≥ 0 and A_z w ≤ e_z pointwise for both z. Interpretation: the largest total mass of an IV-consistent coupling that can be embedded under e without exceeding any observable entry; t* = 1 iff e itself is realizable. (Substitution w = t·q makes the naive bilinear program linear.)
4. **kl_contextuality** (CbG-style relative entropy of contextuality): min over q ∈ Δ(𝕽) of Σ_z KL(e_z ‖ π_z(q)). The objective is convex in q, so it is solved by deterministic EM (multiplicative) updates from the uniform start, iterated to relative change below 1e-13; no multistart and no RNG involved. Values are finite whenever some q gives full pushforward support on both contexts; numerically floored at ε = 1e-300 inside logs.

**Redundancy protocol (F4).** Spearman rank correlation of each witness against slack_l1 over all instances and within the contextual subset; plus a monotone-injective alarm (isotonic R² ≥ 0.9999 on rank space). Output: `witness_lp_redundancy.csv`.

## 6. Nulls, alternatives, strictness scope (C2)

H0(S) for a declared class S: observables lie in P_S (the response polytope of S). The testable content of S_IV consists entirely of inequalities: the latent-projected ADMG of the plain IV model is the chain Z→X→Y, and every positive distribution factorizes trivially as P(z)P(x|z)P(y|x,z); the chain ADMG carries no nested-Markov equality constraints (conditioning on X opens the collider path to the latent, so no conditional independence is implied), consistent with P_BP being full-dimensional with only positivity and inequality facets. Therefore for WP 1.3 the strictness scan records n_nm_constraints = 0 and margins equal to witness magnitudes, and the genuinely informative strictness test (witnesses firing while equality constraints hold with margin) transfers to the atlas structures of WP 1.4 that do possess nontrivial equality constraints. Named alternative classes for later phases (confounder strength ρ families) are out of scope here; Phase 1 needs only the feasibility dichotomy.

## 7. Hand-worked micro-examples (checked by hand, then by machine)

Notation: response table written (r_X(0), r_X(1); r_Y(0), r_Y(1)).

**ME-A (noncontextual, silent witness).** e_0 = δ_{(0,0)}, e_1 = δ_{(1,1)}. Take table (0,1; 0,1): z=0 gives (r_X(0), r_Y(r_X(0))) = (0, r_Y(0)) = (0,0) ✓; z=1 gives (1, r_Y(1)) = (1,1) ✓. Feasible; all witnesses at noncontextual values (t* = 1, slack = 0, KL = 0).

**ME-B (contextual, fired witnesses, hand-complete).** e_0 = δ_{(0,0)}, e_1 = δ_{(0,1)}. Infeasibility, exhaustive: z=0 demands r_X(0) = 0 and r_Y(0) = 0; z=1 demands r_X(1) = 0 and r_Y(r_X(1)) = r_Y(0) = 1; the requirements r_Y(0) = 0 and r_Y(0) = 1 conflict for every one of the 16 tables. Witnesses by hand: cf1_hard = 1/2 (tables may satisfy one context's support, never both, by the same conflict). cf1_soft = 0: A_0w ≤ δ_{(0,0)} confines w to rows with r_X(0)=0, r_Y(0)=0 (four rows), while A_1w ≤ δ_{(0,1)} confines w to rows with r_X(1)=0, r_Y(0)=1 (four rows); the row sets are disjoint, so Σw = 0. slack_l1 = 2: upper bound by q* = δ_{(0,·; 0,·)} giving objective 0 + |δ_{(0,0)} − δ_{(0,1)}|₁ = 2; lower bound via L1 = 4 − 2(π_0(q)(0,0) + π_1(q)(0,1)) and the fact that no single row serves both target entries, so any mixture scores at most 1. kl_contextuality = 2 log 2 ≈ 1.3863: any q misses at least one supported entry per context, so each KL(e_z ‖ π_z(q)) ≥ log 2 on the two supported coordinates, attained by the uniform q. degree_signed = 0 by the constructive quasi-coupling q = δ_a + δ_b − δ_c with a = (0,1; 0,1), b = (1,0; 1,1), c = (1,1; 0,1): Σq = 1, π_0(q) = δ_{(0,0)}, π_1(q) = δ_{(0,1)} exactly. This example permanently documents why degree_signed is not comparable to honest distances.

**ME-C (feasible, on a non-degenerate point).** q* = ½δ_a + ½δ_b with a, b as in ME-B: e_0 = ½δ_{(0,0)} + ½δ_{(1,1)}, e_1 = ½δ_{(1,1)} + ½δ_{(0,1)}. Feasible by construction (realized by q*); slack = 0, t* = 1. Used to discipline tolerances near the boundary.

Machine verification: `tests/test_micro.py` recomputes all of the above independently (LP + exhaustive enumeration + EM-based KL) and asserts agreement with the hand values.

## 8. Sampling and ground-truth protocol for WP 1.3

Uniform stream: Dirichlet(1) per context (seeds 0..99, batches of 10⁴). Boundary stream: feasible points advanced along affine-hull directions and placed at log-spaced fractions of the closed-form ray-exit distance through the exact facet system; plus exact polytope vertices. Sparse-support stream: Dirichlet draws with 1-3 entries zeroed per context, probing the possibilistic tier which requires exact zeros. Facet derivation: the polytope is first reduced exactly to injective affine coordinates (rational left-kernel computation of [Mᵀ | −1]); qhull triangulates the hull of the integer vertex images in those coordinates; every simplicial piece determines its hyperplane exactly through rational nullspace snapping, validated by exact sign checks against all vertices, with duplicates merged by canonical normals. Completeness is cross-checked empirically by the facet-system vs primal-LP agreement audit. Feasibility decisions: primal LP (HiGHS) with tolerance 1e-9; borderline band [1e-9, 1e-7] flagged and excluded from agreement statistics (measure-zero under continuous sampling). All seeds in `configs/seeds.json`; rerun end-to-end under one hour on local CPU.

## 9. Atlas generalization (WP 1.4)

For a general binary structure class: response coordinates per endogenous node, observable vector P(o) or conditional blocks, linear map M_S, identical witnesses. Methodological notes locked before coding (WP 1.4): (a) the generic joint builder treats every root coordinate as freely coupled, which silently drops root independence; structures whose testable content relies on an exogenous instrument must therefore be parametrized in CONDITIONAL form (blocks P(x,y|z), response coordinates excluding Z). A1 uses the S_IV map itself; A2_iv_direct uses a conditional map with r_Y of domain (x,z). (b) For the pure collider Z→X←U→Y, Y's mechanism depends on the unobserved U directly, so the table-simplex reduction fails and the feasible set is a finite union of polytopes (nonconvex); feasibility is decided exactly by a mixed-integer program over the 8 effective cell types (f_0, f_1, g) with shared cell masses across contexts. (c) A4_bow uses the joint form (no randomization assumption to encode). Structures whose obstruction set vanishes (feasible set = whole simplex) are reported as such rather than forced into witness analyses.
